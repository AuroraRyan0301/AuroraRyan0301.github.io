We expose a long-overlooked negative correlation between the scattering
and transmittance components of the extinction gradient, and exploit
it through a principle we call **sample matching**: evaluate both
components at a single shared probe. The matched estimator is built on
the first reformulation of the differential path integral that couples
the two terms inside a single integrand, and reduces gradient variance
by up to **80%** over DRT on voxel-grid reconstruction.
