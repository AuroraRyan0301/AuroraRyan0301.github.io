Hi, I'm **Ruihan Yu** (Aurora). I am an **M2 student** in
[**Umetani Lab**](https://www-ui.is.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~ume/) at
The University of Tokyo, working on physics-based differentiable rendering.

I am fortunate to collaborate with Prof. **Feng Xu**
(Tsinghua University) and Prof. **Shuang Zhao**
(University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign), and to have had a wonderful
internship under Prof. **Hao Su** at UC San Diego.

My research focuses on **differentiable Monte Carlo rendering of
participating media**: designing unbiased estimators for the radiative
transfer equation that converge faster and reconstruct cleaner,
physically meaningful volumetric scenes.
